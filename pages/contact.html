FAQ

Common Questions

What types of fiber can be used for sensing?

Our system works with standard single-mode telecommunications fiber. In many cases, existing "dark fiber" in telecom networks can be repurposed for sensing without affecting data traffic on adjacent fibers.

How long does installation typically take?

For existing fiber, deployment can be completed in days. For new fiber installations, timelines depend on the infrastructure type and access requirements. We work closely with customers to minimize disruption.

What is the typical cost compared to point sensors?

DFOS becomes increasingly cost-effective as monitoring length increases. For applications requiring continuous coverage over kilometers, DFOS typically provides 60-80% cost savings compared to equivalent point sensor deployments.

Can you integrate with existing monitoring systems?

Yes. Our platform provides standard APIs and data export capabilities. We can integrate with existing SCADA systems, asset management platforms, and alerting infrastructure.
