The Challenge

Infrastructure Monitoring Is Broken

Critical infrastructure failures cause billions in damages annually. Current monitoring approaches leave dangerous blind spots.

Current Problems

  • Point sensors create coverage gaps between measurement locations
  • Manual inspections are infrequent and miss developing issues
  • Traditional sensors degrade and require costly maintenance
  • Retrofitting existing infrastructure is expensive and disruptive
  • No real-time alerting for sudden structural changes
  • Data silos prevent comprehensive infrastructure analysis

Our Solution

  • Continuous sensing along the entire fiber length—no gaps
  • Real-time monitoring with millisecond response times
  • Passive optical fiber requires minimal maintenance
  • Leverages existing telecommunications fiber infrastructure
  • Instant alerts when anomalies are detected
  • Unified platform for all infrastructure types

Applications

Protecting Critical Infrastructure

Our technology adapts to monitor diverse infrastructure types, each with specialized detection algorithms.

Roads & Highways

Traffic flow analysis, pavement condition monitoring, and subsidence detection for transportation networks.

Pipelines

Leak detection, third-party interference alerts, and integrity monitoring for oil, gas, and water pipelines.

Buildings

Structural health monitoring, seismic response analysis, and foundation settlement tracking.

Bridges

Strain monitoring, load assessment, cable tension analysis, and early warning systems for structural issues.

Tunnels

Ground movement detection, water ingress monitoring, and structural safety assessment.

Power Infrastructure

Cable monitoring, transformer health assessment, and grid stability analysis.
