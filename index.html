The Challenge

Infrastructure Risk Is Rising

Aging assets and climate extremes demand monitoring that scales. Current approaches leave dangerous gaps in both time and space.

Current Problems

  • Inspections are periodic—events happen between samples
  • Point sensors miss local damage—need distributed coverage
  • Current DFOS systems cost $80-100k+—adoption is limited
  • More data isn't valuable unless it drives decisions
  • Owners don't know how to operationalize continuous spatial data
  • Complex ecosystem (owners, consultants, operators) slows adoption

Our Solution

  • Affordable BOTDR hardware unlocks distributed sensing
  • End-to-end workflow: deployment playbook to interpretation
  • Decision-ready outputs—not just data, actionable engineering results
  • Expert analysis converts spatial data into validated insights
  • Co-define thresholds and response workflows with owners
  • Cloud platform + AI screening reduces analysis burden

Applications

Protecting Critical Infrastructure

Our technology adapts to monitor diverse infrastructure types, each with specialized detection algorithms.

Roads & Highways

Traffic flow analysis, pavement condition monitoring, and subsidence detection for transportation networks.

Pipelines

Leak detection, third-party interference alerts, and integrity monitoring for oil, gas, and water pipelines.

Buildings

Structural health monitoring, seismic response analysis, and foundation settlement tracking.

Bridges

Strain monitoring, load assessment, cable tension analysis, and early warning systems for structural issues.

Tunnels

Ground movement detection, water ingress monitoring, and structural safety assessment.

Power Infrastructure

Cable monitoring, transformer health assessment, and grid stability analysis.
